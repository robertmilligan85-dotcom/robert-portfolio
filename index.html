About & Contact

I'm a video producer, technical director, and editor based in Prescott, Arizona.

My background includes municipal television, public-access broadcasting, educational media, live events, documentary production, and independent filmmaking.

I've worked across the entire production process, from developing concepts and setting up equipment to directing cameras, managing audio, editing footage, and delivering finished programs.

I'm comfortable working independently on complex productions as well as coordinating camera operators and technical crews during live events. My experience also includes broadcast systems, classroom audiovisual support, troubleshooting, and live production infrastructure.

I enjoy projects that require a combination of creative thinking, technical problem-solving, and hands-on execution.
